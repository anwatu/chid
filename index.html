Resurgence

A knee-friendly plan built around your office hours, your Tuesday and Thursday classes, and your 6 a.m. runs on Tuesday and Friday. It's designed for someone who tends to start strong and then fall off for weeks. Every day has a small minimum so that stops happening.

    The playbook

    Sessions

    Sets × reps change with the phase. Every move is chosen to load the knee without pounding it. If a rep pushes knee pain past 3 out of 10, use the easier version listed under it.

    Ground rules

    Knee & consistency

    The knee pain scale

    Rate the knee from 0 to 10 during and after every session. Some discomfort is allowed. Pain that's still worse the next morning means you did too much.

    0–3Green. Train as written.
    4–5Yellow. Cut range or load, or swap to the easier version. Bike instead of running.
    6+Red. Stop that movement today. If it's red two sessions in a row, or you see swelling, locking or the knee giving way, book a doctor or physical therapist.

    Comeback rules

    • FloorThe 10-minute floor routine is the minimum every day. On a bad day, doing just that still counts.
    • TwiceNever miss twice. Missing one day happens. Two in a row is how the month-long breaks start.
    • RestartGone 7+ days? Repeat last week's numbers. Gone 3+ weeks? Restart the current phase at its first week. Don't quit the plan.
    • Night beforePack the gym bag Sunday, Tuesday and Thursday nights. Morning hoops start the night before.
    • DeskStand up and walk two minutes every hour at work. Take a 15-minute lunch walk on office days.
    Fuel

    Daily targets

    Weight loss comes mostly from what you eat. Aim for 1 to 1.5 lb a week. That gets you to about 235 by week 12 without wrecking your energy for hoops.
